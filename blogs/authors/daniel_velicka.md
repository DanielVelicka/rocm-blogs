Daniel Velicka is a Research Assistant in the INFRA lab at IT4Innovations, the National Supercomputing Center of the Czech Republic.  
He specializes in energy-efficient high-performance computing (HPC), with a focus on GPGPU energy efficiency. He developed a methodology to measure frequency transitions on accelerators 
and is now implementing this methodology on AMD GPUs. Additionally, he is participating in the development of the [Mandelbrot benchmark](https://code.it4i.cz/jansik/mandelbrot), 
currently implementing support for AMD GPUs. He holds a master’s degree in Applied Mathematics from VSB – Technical University of Ostrava.
